What the platform guarantees

Rules enforced where curl arrives, not where buttons hide.

Every guarantee below is checked on the server for the web interface and the public API alike, and each one has tests behind it.

01

Deadlines that hold

Windows are checked on server time after the row is locked. A late edit is refused with a reason, whichever door it comes through.

403 window_closed
02

Judges who can't peek

A judge reads their own scores and their own in-track assignments. Another judge's ballot is refused by the backend, not hidden by a template.

403 forbidden · tested per role
03

Scores that are calibrated

Harsh and generous judges are corrected with a documented additive model, with its strength chosen by cross-validation, not by taste.

see JUDGING.md
04

Results you can verify

Each publication stores its exact inputs and a digest. One click recomputes the ranking and proves nothing changed after the fact.

sha256 input digest
One event, start to finish

From open call to published verdict.

The same five stages every organizer runs, with the awkward cases handled: duplicate submissions, unfinished review batches, the judge who scores everything the same.

  1. Open

    Dates, tracks, prizes and custom questions, configured by the organizer.

  2. Submit

    Teams form by invite link and edit drafts until the deadline, with a receipt for every revision.

  3. Assign

    Judges are matched by track and conflicts, algorithmically or in batches, with a preview first.

  4. Judge

    A keyboard-first console with autosave, and a live progress board for the organizer.

  5. Publish

    Raw and normalized rankings side by side, then a verifiable publication and CSV exports.

Built for every seat at the table

One portal, four kinds of user.

Roles are scoped to each event, and a person holds exactly one role per event, so nobody judges a contest they compete in or run.

Organizers
  • Weighted rubrics that lock at the first submitted review
  • Live progress: who hasn't started, which projects lack reviews
  • CSV at every stage, and a full JSON export to leave any time
Judges
  • Evidence beside the rubric, number keys to score
  • Autosaved drafts and an explicit submit
  • Only your own assignments, only your own tracks
Participants
  • Teams by invite link, drafts until the deadline
  • A receipt for every submitted revision
  • Feedback released after results are published
