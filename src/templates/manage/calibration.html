5. What this does and does not prove

  • The projects, teams, judges, reviews and scores here are synthetic. Nothing on this page comes from a real submission or a real judge.
  • The judge-to-project routing is deliberate, not random: {{ calibration.design }} Raw means are therefore wrong in a known direction before the engine sees anything.
  • λ = {{ calibration.lam }} {% if calibration.lambda_source == "auto" %}chosen by the predeclared 5-fold cross-validation on these reviews{% else %}pre-declared for this event{% endif %}, and the fit is the same one the results page uses. The numbers follow the current included set: excluding a review above changes them, because that is what excluding a review means.
  • A recovery here shows the method works when its assumptions hold — a known truth, a connected judge-project graph, habits that are roughly constant per judge. It is not a claim that every real event is fixed by normalization, and a failure on another event would be a reason to look at the assumptions, not at the data. The argument and its limits are written out in docs/NORMALIZATION-PROOF.md, regenerated from the organizers' fixtures.json by scripts/normalization_proof.py.
