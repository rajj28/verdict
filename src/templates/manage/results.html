The judge who marks everything the same

A constant scorer adds no ordering information: every project they review moves the same way. They are kept, because their level is absorbed by their offset, and their reviews only pull their own projects toward a common value. Excluding one is an organizer decision that needs a reason, and it is listed in the decision record.
